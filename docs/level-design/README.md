## About the source maps
The VGMaps atlas lists four Intellivision games (seven map images) 29 ColecoVision games (32 map images), and about 850 NES games (thousands of maps). The maps are fan-made
rips credited to their creators, and the games are © their publishers, so this repo links to them
instead of copying them. Open the links in your browser while designing.

The notes in `design-patterns.md` `coleco-to-intellivision.md` and `nes-to-intellivision.md` come from general knowledge of these games plus the image sizes
the atlas reports. They were NOT checked against the map images themselves, because the images
could not be downloaded in the session that wrote them. Treat them as a starting checklist.
